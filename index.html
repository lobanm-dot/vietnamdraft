Numbers 1-195 were officially called for induction in 1970.

What was the draft lottery?
To make the draft seem more fair, the US used a televised lottery. 366 plastic capsules, each containing a birth date, were mixed in a glass container and drawn by hand.


Did everyone with a low number serve?
No. While a low number meant you were called, many men received deferments (for college, essential jobs, or hardship), failed the physical/medical exams, or found other legal (and sometimes illegal) ways to avoid serving.


Why weren't high numbers drafted?
The military only needed a certain number of soldiers each year. Once they met their quota by drafting the lowest numbers (up to 195 in 1970), they stopped calling people.


How accurate is this simulation?
The lottery number for your birthday is a historical fact. However, the exact branch, job, and deployment shown above are a classroom simulation. They are based on realistic percentages of where men were assigned during the war, but individual outcomes varied wildly based on skills, test scores, and timing.
